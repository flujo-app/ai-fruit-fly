    How it works: each fly has its own copy of the real learning centre from the FlyWire map (5,177 Kenyon cells and their connections to the "approach" and "avoid" output neurons).
    At every spot where more than one token is legal, the fly smells "which object am I describing + which blank + this token" and picks the token it likes most, with some randomness.
    Each round, every fly writes 4 programs. The best one earns sugar and the worst one bitter, which changes that fly's connections, just as flies learn from food.
    Each lab copies its best flies' memories over its weakest ones, and the labs swap their best fly every 2 rounds.
    Hand-made: the token rules (which tokens are legal where), the number and colour choices on offer, the `knife()` helper and the customer's 8 tests with partial credit.
