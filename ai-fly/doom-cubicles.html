DOOM GYM · OPEN-PLAN EDITION

Doom Cubicles

1,024 fruit flies sit at 1,024 desks and take turns at Doom. Each player has a complete fly brain: the picture goes into its eyes, and its own steering, walking and jump neurons work the controller. You are the supervisor.

  • WASD to walk, Shift to run, the mouse to look around
  • The big screen on the deck shows the games that are running and the best game so far; PLAY and PAUSE are on the console
  • A blue marker floats over every desk that is playing right now. Walk up to one and watch
  • When a fly kills all 8 monsters, follow the path on the floor to it, then press 1 for a sugar cube or 2 for a management contract
  • 3 shows the best game so far, R records a video of what you see
