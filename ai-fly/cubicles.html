FLY GYM · OPEN-PLAN EDITION

Cubicles

1,024 fruit flies, each with its own copy of a real fly's learning centre, sit at 1,024 desks and type a three.js döner app one token at a time. You are the supervisor.

  • WASD to walk, Shift to run, the mouse to look around
  • The big screen on the deck shows the live training; the buttons on the console set the speed
  • When a fly writes a perfect döner, follow the path on the floor to it, then press 1 for a sugar cube or 2 for a management contract
  • R records a video of what you see
